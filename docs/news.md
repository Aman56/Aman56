## 2024

### ICLR Publication: Grokking Circuits
**October 2024**

Co-authored paper on mechanistic interpretability accepted at ICLR 2024. Investigates sparse and dense subnetwork competition in the grokking phenomenon, providing new insights into how neural networks learn to generalize.

[Read Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11873) · [ICLR Link](https://openreview.net/forum?id=grokking) · [Scholar Profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=dtBDI_oAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao)
